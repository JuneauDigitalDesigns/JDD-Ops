Good to know

Questions before a first visit

What happens at a first visit?
Mostly listening. We'll talk through what brought you in, what you've already tried, and what you'd like to feel different — then agree on a first step together. Nothing is decided for you in that room.
Do you take insurance?
We accept most major plans and will verify your coverage before the appointment so you know the cost in advance. For anything not covered we offer transparent self-pay rates.
How soon can I be seen?
Usually within a week, and we hold a small number of evening and Saturday slots for people who can't take time off. If you call, a person will tell you honestly what's actually open.
Is my information private?
Completely. Everything you share stays between you and your practitioner, and we never share records without your written consent.
